What is protect?

protect is a Python package of statistical-disclosure-control (SDC) verbs for tabular data and analytical outputs. It exposes 17 composable functions — value perturbation, generalisation, suppression, and risk reporting — designed for health-research microdata and result-release workflows. This playground runs the package entirely in your browser via Pyodide; nothing leaves your machine.

A synthetic panel-shaped dataset of 200 patients is pre-loaded as df (columns: pid, visit, dob, sex, zip, country, icd, income, cost). Use the Upload file button below to load your own data (CSV, TSV, JSON, Parquet, Excel — also gzipped). Files never leave your browser. Feather/Arrow is not supported here — Pyodide has no WebAssembly build of pyarrow; convert to Parquet first.

Universal arguments

argtypemeaning
unit_idstr | NoneColumn that identifies an individual or unit. When set, share-based selection and per-unit draws operate at the unit level (one decision per person, broadcast to all their rows).
sharefloat in [0,1]Fraction of units (or rows when unit_id is None) to perturb. share=1.0 means "all"; share=0.1 means "10%".
random_stateint | Generator | NoneSeed or numpy Generator for reproducibility.
Caveats & notes
  • Pseudonymisation is not anonymisation — see BACKGROUND.md for the long version.
  • Default scales (e.g. noise(scale="auto")) are conservative starting points, not policy recommendations.
  • risk reports k-anonymity / uniqueness on the supplied quasi-identifiers only; combine with suppress and the value-level verbs for release.
  • protect applies a recipe in dictionary order — composition matters.
  • Re-run the same code with different random_state values to see noise-driven variability.
