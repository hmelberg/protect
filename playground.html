Welcome to the protect playground

protect is a Python package of statistical-disclosure-control (SDC) verbs for tabular data and analytical outputs. It exposes 17 composable functions — value perturbation, generalisation, suppression, and risk reporting — designed for health-research microdata and result-release workflows.

This playground runs the package entirely in your browser via Pyodide; nothing leaves your machine. A synthetic panel of 200 patients is pre-loaded as df (columns: pid, visit, dob, sex, zip, country, icd, income, cost). Use Upload to load your own data (CSV, TSV, JSON, Parquet, Excel — also gzipped).

Edit the code in the editor, then press Run (or Ctrl/⌘+Enter). The panels below show the original data, the transformed result, a side-by-side diff, and summary statistics. Click any verb on the right to drop a working snippet into the editor.
